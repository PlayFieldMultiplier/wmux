### Fixed

- **Ended tasks no longer block new fan-outs after a restart.** Right after
  wmux started, with many workspaces of finished tasks still open, a bot or
  agent could be refused a fan-out with "at most 8 fan-out tasks may be live
  at once (96 live now)", because those finished tasks were counted as
  running. This happened while the background service was still recovering
  its terminals. wmux now checks which tasks are really running before it
  counts. While it still cannot tell, a finished task no longer holds a
  slot, unless an agent was last seen running in it. (#2387)
