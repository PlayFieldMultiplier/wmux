### Fixed

- **Closing a task with an open worker pane no longer fails once with
  "Permission denied" on Windows.** The close stops the panes in the task's
  worktree and then removes it; a worker process still leaving the folder made
  the first removal fail, and only a second close succeeded. A removal refused
  that way is now tried again for a few seconds before the worktree is kept.
  (#2412)
