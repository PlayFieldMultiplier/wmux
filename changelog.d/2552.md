### Added

- **Bots can read from the services you signed in to.** A bot's research worker can now use a connection you signed in to in Plugins › Connectors (Notion, Google, Slack and the rest). Before this, a signed-in connection reached only your own terminals. The worker reaches the service through wmux, which keeps your sign-in to itself and lets the worker use only the tools you picked for that connection. Such a task has no web search, no network for its commands and no repository. If the sign-in changes or wmux restarts, the bot tells you, and the worker starts again in a new pane once you resume it. macOS and Linux only for now (#2552)
