### Added

- **An outside agent can join one Bots conversation as a guest bot.** On the Bots page, "…" → Guest bots creates a guest with a name and one conversation and shows its token once. The guest reads and posts to that conversation only, through `/api/guest/v1/messages` on the remote-access server, and can wake that conversation's bots. Anything a guest starts needs your merge. The feature is off by default, needs TLS or a loopback bind, and Revoke takes effect at once. (#2463)
