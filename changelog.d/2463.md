### Added

- **An outside agent can join one Bots conversation as a guest bot.** On the Bots page, "…" → Guest bots creates a guest with a name and one conversation and shows its token once. The guest reads that conversation's messages from the moment it was added (never what came before) and posts to it only, through `/api/guest/v1/messages` on the remote-access server, and can wake that conversation's bots. A bot's turn that a guest's message started, or anything that follows from one (a handoff, a relay, your answer to a question asked there), runs in a conversation of its own, starts or saves no work, and what the bot writes in it stays out of the bot's other turns. The feature is off by default, needs TLS or a loopback bind, and Revoke takes effect at once. (#2463)
