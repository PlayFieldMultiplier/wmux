### Changed

- **Data a signed-in task read stays in the bot's own conversation.** Before, a bot whose conversation took in a signed-in task's result could pass it on in many ways: start another worker, write to one, hand work to another bot or mention it, post in General, edit files in its folder, or save a routine or a job.
  - Now that conversation answers only you, in the bot's 1:1.
  - In that conversation the bot starts no other task, writes to no other worker, hands nothing to another bot, and posts nowhere else.
  - It also runs no commands or file edits there.
  - Each refusal tells the bot why, so it can tell you.
- **No standing instructions from a conversation that took in outside text.** A bot no longer saves a routine, a job or a workflow from such a conversation, even when you said yes to it there. A routine runs later as clean work, and your answer does not make what the conversation read clean.

### Fixed

- **A held fan-out with a signed-in connection starts when a worker slot frees.** Before, its retry was always refused.
- **A task's record keeps which of its connections were signed in.** Before, the record dropped them, so none of the checks for signed-in tasks applied.
