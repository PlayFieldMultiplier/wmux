### Fixed

- **A Claude pane keeps its resume pill even when Gemini was mentioned.** A
  line that merely named Gemini — a Claude reply comparing models,
  `cd ~/gemini` — could make the screen detector take the pane over, and
  after a daemon restart the pane then had no resume pill at all. Gemini is
  now recognised only by its own screen — its composer placeholder, its
  footer or its update notice — and when the detector's last guess is an
  agent that cannot be resumed, the pill falls back to the agent of the
  saved conversation. (A pane where Gemini really ran last after Claude is
  offered Claude's conversation, since Gemini has nothing to resume.) The
  Codex case — a Claude pane whose detector flipped to Codex — is not
  changed by this: Codex can be resumed, so the detector's later word still
  wins. (#2389)
