### Fixed

- **A Claude pane keeps its resume pill even when Gemini was mentioned.** A
  line that merely named Gemini — a Claude reply comparing models,
  `cd ~/gemini` — could make the screen detector take the pane over, and
  after a daemon restart the pane then had no resume pill at all (or, for a
  Codex mention, offered Codex's session picker instead of your Claude
  conversation). Gemini is now recognised only by its own screen — its
  composer placeholder, footer or update notice — and when the detector's
  last guess is an agent that cannot be resumed, the pill falls back to the
  saved conversation's agent. (#2389)
