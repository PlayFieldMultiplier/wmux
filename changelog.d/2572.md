### Added

- **Agents can send images with their messages, to a pane on this PC or on another one.** `send_message` takes up to four PNG, JPEG or WebP files (5 MB each, 10 MB per message) from the pane's project folder or `~/.wmux/uploads/`. The receiving PC keeps its own copy in `~/.wmux/uploads/a2a/` for 7 days. The receiving agent's message ends with the path of each image, so it can open them. Metadata such as a photo's location is removed before an image leaves, and only real image files are accepted, whatever their name says. Cross-PC messages carried text only before. A PC whose wmux is older gets the text with a note that images were left out. (#2569, #2571, #2572)
