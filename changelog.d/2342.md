### Fixed

- **Starting many WSL workers at once works.** When eight or so tasks started
  together in WSL, the last one could fail with a false "the directory no
  longer exists", and its folder was left behind. Some workers also came up
  without ever running their agent, and nobody was told. Slow WSL spawns are
  now waited for, a worker's launch line is sent once more when the shell
  never read it, and a worker that still never starts is reported as needing
  you. (#2342)
