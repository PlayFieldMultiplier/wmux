### Fixed

- **wmux starts on minimal Linux installs.** The Linux .deb and .rpm now
  require the ALSA sound library, so wmux starts on minimal installs. Before,
  installing the .deb with `--no-install-recommends`, in a container or on a
  minimal image, or installing the .rpm without ALSA already present, left out
  libasound.so.2 and wmux failed to start. (#2786)
