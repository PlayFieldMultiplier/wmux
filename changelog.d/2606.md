### Changed

- **The morning report no longer lists the titles of other bots' 1:1 threads with you.** They still count as open threads. Before, the Coordinator's report showed each open 1:1's title, which is your first message there.
- **A bot's wake no longer picks up replies from another bot's 1:1 with you.** Before, a room-wide message could be matched to a card in another bot's 1:1, and the replies after it there could reach the woken bot.
