### Changed

- When a bot's conversation has read outside text (a pull request, an issue, CI), your own request to set up a routine or to remember something is no longer refused. It goes to an approval card that shows your message and the whole change, and only your Approve on the desktop card saves it. A profile change asked there uses the same card. These cards cannot be approved from the phone, the "Approve N pending" chip or typed words, and they have no Undo.

### Notes

- Going back to an older build while such a request is in `approvals.json` keeps approvals off in that build until the request is settled and dropped.
