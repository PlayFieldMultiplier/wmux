### Changed

- **Settings puts things where you look for them.** Terminal font size, font and cursor shape moved from Appearance to the top of Terminal, and the Plugins tab in Settings, which only linked to the Plugins page, is gone. On macOS and Linux, Settings no longer shows text written for Windows: the agy accounts section appears only on Windows, and rows that work everywhere now use your system's names (Finder, the Dock, ⌘G). Search follows the same rules. A switch that is off now disables the rows that depend on it, such as the pane flash under the pane ring and the Bots tuning rows while the bots are off. The account rotation checkboxes are now switches, and the Keyboard list no longer shows the Company View shortcut, which has done nothing since #503. (#2835)
